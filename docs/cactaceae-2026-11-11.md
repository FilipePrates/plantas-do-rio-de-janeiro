## Botanical decisions

- Current Flora e Funga names are used. *Pereskia grandifolia* retains *Rhodocactus grandifolius* as a searchable alternative.
- *Hatiora salicornioides* follows Flora e Funga; iNaturalist and POWO use *H. salicornoides*. The spelling difference is explicit and each photo keeps its original observation identification.
- *Rhipsalis paradoxa* uses the regional treatment for its winged segments; a conflicting WFO summary is documented in curation notes.
- *Cereus sericifer* is accepted separately in the current Flora service but retained as a subspecies of *C. fernambucensis* by other treatments. No unsupported second illustrated profile was made from the same concept.
- *Cereus hildmannianus* was deferred because the regional revision questions the historical voucher. *Schlumbergera russelliana* was deferred because the only eligible observation provided weak views of the flowers. These decisions do not modify the supplied workbook.

Descriptions are original Portuguese summaries, with exact source links in each profile.

The supplied workbook remains the state authority for listed species; additional epiphytic/restinga species have state-level records in their cached Flora records and regional treatments. No municipal scope was inferred from photographs. Foreign photographs illustrate morphology and do not establish a Rio locality. No food, medicinal or cultivation recommendation was inferred.
